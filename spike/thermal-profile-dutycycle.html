Plan ref: D27, R11, §18.2 — Validates duty-cycle economics.
Purpose: Confirm 50% duty-cycling roughly halves heat for roughly half the rate, and completes where 100% duty may not.
Protocol: Process frames for 1 second, skip for 1 second (50% duty cycle).
Expected outcome: Temperature and decode rate should be ~50% of baseline, but transfer completes successfully.
