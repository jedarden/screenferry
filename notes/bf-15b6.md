## Implementation Details

### Core Module: `src/platform/capture-resolution.ts`

**Resolution Profiles (§6.4 measurements):**
- **720p** (1280×720): 100% erasure at 1.5 camera px/module - NOT RECOMMENDED
- **1080p** (1920×1080): Best goodput at 2.25 camera px/module - RECOMMENDED DEFAULT

## Problem Solved

The original issue: *"getUserMedia defaults are NOT adequate — a Pixel 6 defaults to 1080 on the SHORT edge"*

**Solution:** Explicit resolution constraints ensure consistent behavior:
- `{ideal: 1920, ideal: 1080}` for 1080p (long edge)
- `{ideal: 1280, ideal: 720}` for 720p
- `{ideal: 3840, ideal: 2160}` for 4K

This prevents devices from defaulting to 1080p on the short edge (which would be 1080×1920 portrait).
